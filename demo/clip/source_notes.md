Source notes for the Riverside Community Garden watering guide (invented example)

- The garden has 21 raised beds and two shared tool sheds.
- Team A waters the east beds on Monday and Thursday.
- Team B waters the west beds on Tuesday and Friday.
- On Saturday anyone who signed up on the board waters all beds.
- City water rule: hoses may be used only before 9 am.
- Turn off the main valve when you leave.
- Log each watering on the board by the gate.
- Reach the garden coordinator by leaving a note on the gate board.
- Tool shed keys come from the coordinator.
