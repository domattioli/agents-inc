Riverside Community Garden: watering guide

Overview
The garden has 12 raised beds and two shared tool sheds. This guide explains who waters, when, and how.

Schedule
Monday and Thursday: east beds, team A.
Tuesday and Friday: west beds, team B.
Saturday: all beds, whoever signed up on the board.

Rules
Hoses may not be used before 9 am.
Turn off the main valve when you leave.
Log each watering on the board by the gate.

Contacts
Garden coordinator: leave a note on the gate board.
Tool shed keys: ask the coordinator.
